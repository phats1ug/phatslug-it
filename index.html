About Me

I'm getting back into IT after spending many years away from the field. Over the years I've often found myself acting as the unofficial "IT person" in schools, helping colleagues troubleshoot hardware, software and everyday technical problems.

Formal IT support was sometimes only available on a rotating basis, so I developed a practical, hands-on approach to solving problems and helping people get back up and running.

I'm now building on that experience through structured learning and practical projects, using my own hardware and homelab to put what I'm learning into practice.
